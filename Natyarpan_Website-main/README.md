# 🎭 Natyarpan

**Natyarpan** is a full-stack website developed as the official website of the RIT College Drama Club, Ishwarpur. It showcases the club's Marathi theatre productions, highlights the students behind them, and provides a platform to celebrate their creativity, talent, and contributions to the performing arts.

🌐 **Live Site:** https://natyarpan-website.vercel.app/

💻 **GitHub:** https://lnkd.in/d8Mu5yWM

## ✨ Features

- **Cinematic Home Page:** An engaging landing page with a video showcase of the club's work.
- **Past Shows Section:** Details of current productions like Halfway and Ukali, including date, time and venue.
- **Unfold Legacy Archive:** A collection of plays from 2017 to 2025, each with a short synopsis.
- **Team Section:** Introduces the student artists and their roles in acting, direction, lighting, music, costumes and set design.
- **Digital Ticket:** A scratch-to-reveal ticket that visitors can claim and download.
- **Contact and Social Links:** Quick access to the club's email, WhatsApp group and Instagram page.
- **Responsive Design:** Works across different screen sizes.
